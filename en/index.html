02 / Understanding the process

Pyrolysis in a few steps

The material is heated without oxygen. Liquids, gas and a solid are then recovered.

Prepare

Sort, adjust the size and prepare the material.

Transform

Heat the material in a reactor.

Separate

Cool the vapours and collect the products.

Selected plastics

Pyrolysis oil for a defined industrial use; gas and solid residue to be characterised.

Selected biomass

Biochar, gas and liquids: the split depends on the material and the chosen process.

Biochar: a plant-based charcoal rich in stable carbon. Used in soils or materials, it stores carbon over the long term.

Key takeaway

The product obtained depends on the chosen material and process.

07 / Carbon & traceability

CO₂: a value to qualify for each stream

Biomass and plastics follow different mechanisms. Their certification is prepared from the study stage.

Biomass → Biochar

A carbon removal can be certified if the method, the measurements and the use of the biochar meet the criteria.

Plastics → Circular material

ISCC PLUS organises traceability and mass balance. This certification is not a CO₂ credit.

Qualify→Measure→Have it verified→Contract

Where do you fit in?

This agreement shows that major buyers commit for 10 years. The biochar market grew from $14.6M in 2022 to $181.5M in 2024. A biomass unit enters it as a supplier of certified credits, at its own scale, once the methodology is validated.

Key takeaway

Biochar and plastics follow two separate routes. No carbon revenue in the scenario.

Nabta Alkon / Transformation chain

The process: three outputs, dedicated circuits

Heat transforms the material. The vapours are cooled; gas and solids follow their own circuits.

Prepare

Sort, shred and adjust moisture as needed.

Reactor

Heat and control the residence time.

Process heat

Gas used depending on the configuration. This heat is returned to the reactor.

Solid

Cool, characterise and package.

Vapours

Condense into liquid, then collect.

Gas

Treat and direct to the intended use.

Simplified functional diagram. Weighing, batch traceability, maintenance and emissions monitoring accompany the process. The gas used for heat reduces an energy need; its value is not counted twice.

Source: [R5] IEA Bioenergy · process ↗

Nabta Alkon / Certification & value

Carbon: a chain of evidence for each stream

First qualify eligibility and use; then measure, verify and contract the corresponding value.

1 Qualify

Material, process and end use.

2 Measure

Batches and monitoring data.

3 Verify

Independent control.

4 Contract

Value, volume and delivery.

Biochar: carbon removal

Biochar can store carbon. Issuing credits follows a methodology, a verified net removal and monitoring of uses.

Plastics: mass balance

ISCC PLUS tracks the allocation of materials in a certified chain. This mechanism is not a CO₂ removal credit.

MRV = measurement, documentation and verification. For biochar, a future sale of credits can be arranged before issuance. Price, fees and eligibility are to be established. The plastics scenario keeps carbon revenue at zero.
